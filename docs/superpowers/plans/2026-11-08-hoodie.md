# Hoodie Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Dress the bust in a hoodie with the hood down, so it is never shown bare, without changing the node contract.

**Architecture:** A new step `art/head/steps/40_hoodie.py` builds the hoodie from the bust itself (CC0 by construction): the skin below a tilted neckline is smoothed and offset outward, a hood roll is swept around the smoothed neckline, and two drawstrings follow the chest. The skin hidden under the cloth is deleted. The hoodie is one static mesh bone-parented to `Bust`. `30_bust.py` records the joints in hero units on `Skin` for this and later steps.

**Tech Stack:** Blender 5.2.1 bmesh, the existing `npm run head` pipeline.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-08-cyborg-head-bust-design.md` ("Goals": hoodie, hood down)

## Global Constraints

- Stylized-real (Cameron, 2026-11-08). Dark hoodie, light drawstrings.
- The neck above the collar keeps bending with `Head`; nothing may poke through at the hero's turn limits.
- Budget unchanged: under 3 MB, at most 40,000 triangles. No em dashes.

## Review Focus

1. Skin poking through the cloth at rest or turned: the four renders show none.
2. The neckline too high (a turtleneck) or too low (shows the chest): tilted from about -1.6 at the front to -1.4 at the back.
3. A lumpy hood roll from the zigzag cut edge: the neckline is smoothed before the sweep.
